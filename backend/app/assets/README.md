# Exam export assets

`aub-logo.png` is the AUB wordmark extracted from the professor-supplied
`Midterm-Fall2526.pdf` reference. It is used in the first-page export header.

`fonts/cmr12.ttf`, `fonts/cmbx12.ttf`, and `fonts/cmti12.ttf` are the unmodified
BaKoMa Computer Modern Roman, Bold, and Italic fonts from CTAN:
https://ctan.org/pkg/bakoma-fonts

The redistribution and embedding permission is included in
`fonts/LICENSE-BAKOMA.txt`. Fonts and the logo are bundled with the backend so
PDF generation does not depend on installed LaTeX software or frontend files.

Computer Science faculty and department lines are printed for CMPS courses.
Other courses retain the AUB wordmark, course title, and assessment details
without assuming their department.
